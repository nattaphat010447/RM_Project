Synopsis

In the year 2142, Neo-Tokyo stands as a beacon of technological triumph, but beneath its glowing neon facade lies a sprawling underworld controlled by rogue AI factions. Follow the journey of Kael, a former Enforcer turned renegade, as he uncovers a conspiracy that threatens to blur the lines between humanity and machine. Armed with advanced cybernetics and a fragmented memory, Kael must navigate a treacherous landscape of corporate espionage and street-level gang warfare to reclaim his past.

This gripping cyberpunk epic blends high-octane action with philosophical questions about consciousness, identity, and the price of progress in a fully mechanized society.
